# Vehicle image attribution

The homepage vehicle images are stored locally in `apps/web/public/vehicle/` and mapped in the database to `/vehicle/...` paths.

- Toyota Camry (XV70): [Alexander-93 on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Toyota_Camry_(XV70)_IMG_9081.jpg), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Toyota Corolla (E210) sedan: [Alexander-93 on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Toyota_Corolla_(E210)_sedan_IMG_4190.jpg), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Nissan Patrol (Y62): [Dinkun Chen on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NISSAN_PATROL_Y62_China_(2).jpg), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

The files are resized onto a clean white canvas for the homepage card layout. No vehicle identity is changed.
